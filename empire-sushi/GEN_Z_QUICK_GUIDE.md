## Strategic Metrics Cards

### Card 1: Gen Z Hotspot Coverage
**What it shows:** Which brands have stores in Gen Z-rich districts (≥50k Gen Z pop)

**How to read:**
```
Brand Name: X/Y stores → Z%
```
- X = Stores in Gen Z hotspots
- Y = Total stores
- Z = Penetration percentage

**Example:**
```
Empire Sushi: 15/45 → 33%
Sushi King: 42/89 → 47%
```
→ Sushi King has better Gen Z coverage

### Card 2: Primary Ethnicity Targeting
**What it shows:** Main ethnic group each brand serves (based on district demographics)

**How to read:**
```
Brand → Ethnicity: N stores (P%)
```
- Ethnicity = Most common in brand's districts
- N = Number of stores in that ethnic majority districts
- P = Percentage of brand's portfolio

**Example:**
```
Empire Sushi → Bumi Malay: 28 (62%)
```
→ 62% of Empire stores are in Malay-majority districts

### Card 3: Empire Sushi Strategic Position
**What it shows:** Empire's key demographic metrics

**Three metrics:**
1. **Gen Z Hotspot Penetration** - % of stores in youth-rich areas
2. **District Coverage** - # of unique districts
3. **Primary Target** - Main ethnic demographic served

## Common Questions

**Q: What age is "Gen Z"?**
A: Ages 10-29 (10-14, 15-19, 20-24, 25-29 age groups)

**Q: What makes a "Gen Z Hotspot"?**
A: District with ≥50,000 Gen Z population

**Q: How is "Primary Ethnicity" determined?**
A: The ethnic group with the highest population in that district (2024 data)

**Q: Why focus on Gen Z?**
A: They're the core sushi-eating demographic - young, urban, adventurous eaters

**Q: How do I compare Empire to competitors?**
A: Look at the strategic metrics cards - they show all brands side-by-side

## Pro Tips

## Example Analysis Workflow

1. **Switch to District Demographics tab**
2. **Select "Gen Z Hotspots"** metric
3. **Scan map** for dark red areas
4. **Check "Gen Z Hotspot Coverage" card**
   - See Empire's 33% vs Sushi King's 47%
   - Goal: Improve Empire's penetration
5. **Identify target districts:**
   - Find dark red districts where Empire = 0 stores
   - But competitors have stores
6. **Switch to "Primary Ethnicity"** metric
7. **Verify ethnic fit:**
   - Check if red districts match Empire's primary target
8. **Result:** List of priority expansion districts
